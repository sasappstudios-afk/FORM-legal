FORM — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

FORM ("we", "us", "the app") is a facial appearance coaching app developed by Sajad Ajam. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is stored, and your rights.

1. Data we collect

  • Face photographs. When you use the Check-in (scan) feature, the app captures photographs of your face using your device's camera. These images are used solely to measure facial appearance metrics within the app.
  • Name. During onboarding you may provide your first name, used only for personalised greetings inside the app.
  • Onboarding answers. Age range, skin-care routine, lifestyle preferences. Used to personalise your coaching plan.
  • Scan scores and history. The measurements and scores the app calculates from your photographs.
  • Purchase information. Subscription status is managed by Google Play Billing. We use RevenueCat as an intermediary to verify your subscription entitlement. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipt from Google Play; it does not receive your name, email, photographs or scan data.

2. Data storage

All photographs, scores, onboarding answers and coaching plans are stored locally on your device only. They are never uploaded to any server, cloud service or third party. If you uninstall the app or delete your data through Settings, this data is permanently removed.

3. Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your email address, phone number or physical address.
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not collect device identifiers or advertising IDs.
  • We do not track your location.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing — processes payments for subscriptions and one-time purchases. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — verifies subscription entitlements. Receives an anonymous user ID and purchase receipt. Governed by RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

No other third-party services receive any data from the app.

5. Camera permission

The app requests camera access to capture face photographs for analysis. The camera is used only during the scan flow and only when you initiate a check-in. No images are transmitted off your device.

6. Notifications

If you opt in, the app may send local reminders for upcoming check-ins. These notifications are generated on-device and do not involve any server.

7. Children's privacy

FORM is not directed at anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Data deletion

You can delete all your data at any time through the app's Settings screen ("Delete data"). Because all data is stored on-device, deletion is immediate and permanent. Uninstalling the app also removes all stored data.

9. Data security

Your data is protected by your device's own security (screen lock, encryption). Since no data leaves your device, there is no transmission to intercept.

10. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, the revised version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
